# ZEROS' Brand Spec

## Direction

Kinetic editorial arcade: a confident gaming marketplace with an ink-dark play surface, warm paper shopping canvas, and bright signal accents. The experience should feel energetic and authored without becoming a neon-tech template.

## Identity assets

- Existing ZEROS' wordmark and gamepad mark in `src/components/Navbar.tsx` and `src/components/Footer.tsx` are preserved as the current identity assets.
- Product imagery remains catalog data from Firestore. Replace seeded placeholder imagery with owned or official assets when available.

## Color roles

- Ink: `#0c1018` — navigation, hero, dark trust surfaces.
- Paper: `#f4f2ed` — primary shopping canvas.
- Paper bright: `#fffdfa` — cards and operational surfaces.
- Violet: `#7c63ff` — brand signal, action emphasis, depth.
- Mint: `#b9f7d8` — positive state, primary CTA, delivery signal.
- Amber: `#f4b860` — rating and attention accent.
- Coral: `#f17b6c` — destructive or wishlist state.

## Typography and layout

Use the existing system stack for zero font-network cost. Headlines use an oversized, tight editorial scale. Metadata uses compact uppercase labels. Body copy stays at readable 16px-equivalent sizing. Use an 8px spacing rhythm with larger 24/32/48px section intervals.

## Motion

Use short CSS transitions for feedback, pointer depth for the hero, restrained card lift, and ambient particles only where they communicate energy. Honor reduced motion and avoid animating every element simultaneously.

## Voice

Direct, local, confident, and useful. Example lines: “Game credit that lands when you need it.” and “Pick it. Pay locally. Play sooner.” Avoid inflated claims, fake social proof, or generic filler.
